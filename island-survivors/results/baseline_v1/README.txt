# Baseline v1 Experiments

## Configuration

- Teams: 2
- Workers per team: 2
- Starting locations: Northwest and Southeast
- Agent: RuleBasedAgent
- Execution: Headless
- Map generation: Seeded
- Simulation randomness: Seeded

## Experiment: 50 episodes

- Experiment seed: 123
- Episodes: 50
- Team 1 wins: 18
- Team 2 wins: 22
- Draws: 10
- Incomplete episodes: 0

## Observations

- Both teams can gather and deposit berries.
- Leaders automatically consume food from team storage.
- Teams are eliminated when their leader dies.
- Team 2 has a marginally higher average survival time
  in this initial experiment.
- More experiments are needed to assess positional bias.
